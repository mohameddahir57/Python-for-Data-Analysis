## 📁 Dataset Description

A multi-sheet or large CSV containing:
- **Transaction Details**: Date, Product, Amount, Discount.
- **Marketing Channels**: Social, Email, Search, Direct.
- **Customer Feedback**: Rating (1-10), Comments.
- **Geography**: Country, City.

## 🎯 Project Lifecycle

### Phase 1: Cleaning & Prep
- Fix those annoying date strings.
- Impute missing ratings based on average feedback.
- Remove duplicate transactions.

### Phase 2: Deep Dive Analysis
- Calculate the ROI (Return on Investment) for different marketing channels.
- Identify the most profitable countries.
- Analyze how discounts affect final sales volume.

### Phase 3: The Master Dashboard
- A 4-panel professional dashboard showing:
  1. Performance Trends.
  2. Profitability Map.
  3. Customer Satisfaction Levels.
  4. Marketing Channel Efficiency.

### Phase 4: Final Presentation
- Write a 1-page summary in Markdown at the end of your notebook.
- Include: Objective, Methodology, Results, and 3 high-impact business recommendations.
